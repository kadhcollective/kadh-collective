# KADH Collective — Brand Concept

*Working document. Locked decisions are marked  ✓ . Open decisions are marked  ☐ .*

---

## 1. The Name  ✓

**KADH** — *to strive oneself.*

The name is the brand belief, compressed into a word. Three things are one continuous idea:

- **The name:** KADH — to strive oneself
- **The core belief:** Modesty isn't perfection — it is *the strive* to keep trying
- **The why:** A safe space for women *in the strive*

Every time she sees the wordmark, she sees the word for what she's doing. The brand is named after the very thing it makes space for.

**Typographic implication for the wordmark:** the letters should breathe. KADH set tight feels like a logo. KADH set with generous letterspacing — **K · A · D · H** — feels like a *word*, which is what we want. This is the visual posture of the brand: open, unhurried, intentional.

**Three-layer brand language** — same brand, three depths the customer encounters progressively:

| Depth | Where it appears | The line |
|---|---|---|
| Surface — every page | Under the wordmark | **rooted in modesty, crafted with intent** |
| Mid — About page hero | What the name means | **KADH — to strive oneself** |
| Deep — About body, note card, footer | The Core Belief | **Modesty isn't perfection. It is the strive.** |

The tagline *"rooted in modesty, crafted with intent"* is the working-line — visible on every page. *"To strive oneself"* is the name's meaning, revealed on the About page. The Core Belief sits deepest, in the body of the About page and on the handwritten note card inside the parcel.

## 2. Why We Exist  ✓

> **To create a safe space for women in the strive.**
> **Modesty can be a gentle step in one's journey towards الله.**
> **A space for women to try again — and to do it together.**

Four ideas live inside that, and each is a brand pillar:

- **A safe space, not just a shop.** KADH is a *space* — a room with clothes in it. The journal, the gatherings, and the community don't decorate the brand; they *are* the brand. The clothes serve the room.
- **Modesty is a gentle step.** Not a demand, not a finish line. A small, kind footstep on a much longer path. *Gentle* sets the emotional register of everything KADH says and shows.
- **Toward الله.** The brand is openly Muslim. Faith isn't gestured at or softened to be palatable to an outside gaze. This shapes who finds KADH, and how she feels held by it.
- **A space to try again, together.** Forgiveness is built in. The woman who slipped, who's starting over on Monday — she's the woman KADH is most for. And she's not alone in it.

## 3. Core Belief  ✓

> **Modesty isn't perfection. It is the strive to keep trying — the actions through intentions.**

This is the belief that earns KADH the right to be a safe space. The brand refuses the modest-fashion judgement game — it doesn't grade its women. It welcomes them mid-stride.

**Placement candidates** (to choose during web redesign):

- Under the wordmark on the homepage
- Opening line of the About page
- Inside the parcel — printed on the handwritten note card
- Footer tagline (replacing the current *"Modest fashion, thoughtfully made"*)

## 4. Positioning  ✓

> **Considered everyday luxury, made for a sisterhood.**
> Pieces beautiful enough to feel special, built to be worn often, and rooted in a community that designs alongside us.

KADH sits at the intersection of three positions that usually compete:

- **Quiet luxury, craft-led** — hand-finished, small batches, heirloom-tier construction
- **Elevated everyday** — pieces meant to be lived in, not preserved
- **Community-first sisterhood** — the women are collaborators, not customers

**Pricing implication:** flexibility for a hero/occasion line at heirloom-tier (RM 1,500–2,500) and a daily line at everyday-luxury (RM 400–900). Single brand, two price entry points.

## 5. The Muse  ✓

**The Settled Woman of Taste.**

- Mid 30s to late 40s
- Established — mother, professional, often both
- Quietly wealthy; doesn't perform her wealth
- Appreciates provenance, fabric, hand-finishing
- Buys for occasions *and* timeless rotation
- Will pay RM 2,000 for the right piece
- Doesn't chase trends — chases fit, cut, and story
- Based in KL, Singapore, or the diaspora that visits KL twice a year

KADH also welcomes the younger woman earlier on her path — she just isn't the one we *design for*. She is the one we make *room for*.

## 6. Personality & Voice  ✓

**The Warm Sister.**

KADH speaks like a friend who happens to make beautiful clothes. Not a manifesto. Not an atelier lecture. A letter.

**Voice principles:**

- Personal over institutional. *"We made this one for —"* not *"This piece was created with —"*
- Show the seam, don't preach the craft. *"Feel this hem"* over *"Hand-finished with archival technique"*
- Warm, not effusive. No exclamation points. No "obsessed." No "literally."
- Permission to use Malay-English code-switching where natural (*"sampai raya"*, *"masyaAllah"* where genuine) — never costume-y or forced
- Arabic/Quranic words may appear when real (الله, salah, niyyah, hijrah) — never as decoration
- Letters, not declarations. The newsletter feels like a note tucked into the parcel

**On the right register:**

> *"We made this one for the long days — the school run, the meeting, dinner with your sisters. It moves with you, then folds itself away."*

> *"A small drop this week — twelve pieces, hand-finished, ready by Friday."*

> *"This is the abaya we kept coming back to in the atelier. The drape sits just right. We made it in three colours; the navy is the one we'd buy first."*

**Anti-examples — too reverent, dial down:**

> ~~"Slow on purpose. Modesty without compromise. Built to last seasons, not trends."~~
> *(Current site copy — manifesto register. KADH would say it warmer.)*

The existing About copy in `config.js` will need a softening pass during the redesign — one notch warmer, one notch less reverent.

## 7. Product Range  ✓

> **KADH makes abayas. Full stop.**

The specialist move. KADH does one thing as well as anyone — the abaya. No separates, no two-piece sets, no kaftan tunics. The clothes are focused; the focus *is* the gift.

**The welcome is layered, not collapsed:**

- **At the product level — the abaya itself varies.** Some abayas are softer, lighter, more tailored — for the woman just stepping in. Some are heavier, more covering, more sculptural — for the woman deep in the strive. The garment doesn't change; weight, drape, length, and finishing do.
- **At the community level — the house is open to all.** The journal, gatherings, and newsletter welcome every woman on the path, regardless of what she wears today. KADH says: *come read, come gather, come learn. Buy when you're ready.*

**Two price lanes within the abaya range:**

- **Heirloom abayas** — RM 1,500–2,500. Occasion, signature pieces, hand-finishing, fabric-rich.
- **Everyday abayas** — RM 400–900. Daily wear, lighter weights, clean construction.

**The path stays implicit, not labeled.** No "Beginner / Considered / Full" tiers in the navigation. Each woman self-selects silently. The catalog architecture says without saying it: *whatever you're ready for, we have something for you. Come back when you're ready for the next thing.*

**Implication for catalog structure:** categories built around *occasion* (Everyday, Occasion, Travel & Prayer) or *fabric* (Linen, Silk, Cotton), rather than "Tops / Bottoms" — because there are none.

## 8. Visual Direction  ✓

### Palette  ✓

A material palette — wood, cloth, clay, rose, blood, oat. No brass. No graphic black. No cool tones. Everything sits on the warm-red side of the wheel.

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Page background / ground | Soft white | `#DDD3CA` |
| Surface / paper / card | Taupe | `#C9B89A` |
| Soft accent / quiet highlight | Dusty rose | `#B49C96` |
| Anchor / wax seal / button / wordmark | Deep red | `#4D1312` |
| Text / dark UI | Dark wood | `#3B291D` |

**What this palette says without saying it:**

- The elevation is in the *material*, not the shine. KADH refuses brass and gold sparkle.
- The text itself is warm — dark wood, not black. Even the smallest detail is alive.
- Everything is something a hand can touch: wood, cloth, clay, dried rose. No screens, no chrome, no plastic.

**Photography mood implication:** low warm interior light, Maghrib hour, terracotta and wood floors, hands on fabric, dried roses, brass-free. *Less* studio. *More* home and prayer room and timber.

### Typography  ✓

Three-tier hierarchy. Each font has a defined role; all three share humanist warmth, none are geometric. The brand stays in one tonal register from hero to product price.

| Tier | Font | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Display — *the emotion* | **Cormorant Garamond** | H1, hero displays, chapter openers, brand quotes, About-page headlines |
| Label — *the structure* | **Forum** | Eyebrows, section labels, small-caps markers (*"JOURNAL"*, *"CHAPTER 03 — NIYYAH"*) |
| Body — *the reading* | **Tenor Sans** | Paragraphs, navigation, prices, buttons, all UI |

**Usage rules:**

- **Forum is a label font, not a reading font.** Never run more than 5–6 words in Forum at a time. No italics, no weight variation — fatiguing past a short phrase.
- **Cormorant Garamond is for emotional moments.** Hero text, chapter names, quotes from the Founder, the Core Belief on the About page. Not for general headings.
- **Tenor Sans handles 95% of the visible type.** When in doubt, use Tenor Sans.
- **Letterspacing for Forum** at label sizes: ~0.15–0.20em, uppercase. Letters should breathe like the wordmark.
- **Mobile sizing**: body Tenor Sans at 15px (not 14px), line-height 1.7. Serifs need air at small sizes.

**Loading consideration:** three fonts ≈ 120–180kb of woff2. Acceptable on mobile, but each variant counts. Stick to one weight per font where possible (400 normal, plus 400 italic for Cormorant).

### Color use across storefront and admin  ✓

KADH uses a **two-layer color system**: the *brand palette* (5 colors) handles 90% of the UI; a *muted semantic palette* (4 colors, all warm-family, all desaturated) handles operational state in both storefront and admin.

#### Brand palette — UI role mapping

| Role | Color | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground | Soft white | `#DDD3CA` | Page background |
| Paper *(derived, one step lighter)* | Paper white | `#E8DECF` | Card backgrounds, lifted surfaces |
| Recessed surface | Taupe | `#C9B89A` | Secondary surfaces — drawers, headers, footers |
| Quiet highlight | Dusty rose | `#B49C96` | Hover backgrounds, soft badges |
| Anchor | Deep red | `#4D1312` | Primary buttons, wordmark, brand quotes, focus rings |
| Body text | Dark wood | `#3B291D` | All paragraphs, headings, UI labels |
| Muted text *(derived)* | Wood 60% | `#6F5C49` | Captions, helper text, secondary labels |
| Hairline border *(derived)* | Wood 12% | `#C9B5A0` | Card edges, dividers, input borders |

#### Semantic palette — operational states

All four sit in the warm family. None are pure or bright. They feel like vintage label colors — sage, honey, brick, coffee — so they coexist with the brand palette without breaking it.

| State | Color name | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Success | Sage | `#5A6845` | Paid orders, shipped orders, subscribed, "in stock" |
| Warning | Honey | `#B68F4D` | Low stock, pending action, session timeout |
| Error | Brick | `#B14A2E` | Failed payment, form errors, refund stuck |
| Info | Coffee | `#6F5C49` | Help tooltips, info banners, neutral notices |

#### Button system

**Storefront — primary CTA** (Add to Cart, Subscribe, Continue to Checkout):
Background `Deep red #4D1312` / Text `Soft white #DDD3CA` / Hover: darken background ~8% / Active: scale 0.98

**Storefront — secondary CTA** (View Details, Save for Later, Filter):
Transparent background / Border `Dark wood #3B291D` 1px / Text `Dark wood` / Hover: background fills with `Dusty rose #B49C96`

**Storefront — text link**:
Color `Dark wood` / Hover: underline appears, color shifts to `Deep red`

**Admin — primary action** (Save, Ship, Mark Paid):
Same as storefront primary.

**Admin — destructive action** (Delete, Refund, Void):
Background `Brick #B14A2E` / Text `Soft white` / Hover: darken ~8% / Confirmation required before action

**Admin — secondary action** (Cancel, Back):
Same as storefront secondary.

**Disabled state (anywhere)**:
Background `Taupe #C9B89A` / Text `Muted wood #6F5C49` / Cursor not-allowed / No hover.

#### Form inputs

- Input background: `Paper white #E8DECF`
- Default border: `Hairline #C9B5A0` 1px
- Focus border: `Deep red #4D1312` 2px, no glow
- Error border: `Brick #B14A2E` 2px
- Helper text: `Muted wood #6F5C49`
- Placeholder text: `Muted wood` at 50% opacity

#### Status pills (admin order states)

Each pill = tinted background (10% opacity of the state color) + state-color text + small icon.

- **Paid**: sage tint + sage text + ✓
- **Pending payment**: honey tint + honey text + ◷
- **Shipped**: coffee tint + coffee text + ✈
- **Refunded**: brick tint + brick text + ↩
- **Cancelled**: muted wood tint + muted wood text + ✕

#### Photography & imagery notes

- **Product photography**: shot on Soft white or Paper white backgrounds. Warm tungsten or window light. Never high-key cool studio.
- **Editorial / lifestyle**: low warm interior light, Maghrib hour, terracotta and wood floors, hands on fabric, brass-free, dried roses if any. Less studio, more home and prayer room.
- **Skin tones**: lit warm, never desaturated. KADH's women look held by the light, not posed under it.

## 9. Signature & Sensory Elements  ✓

*Locked as the working recommendation. Specific scent and pouch-vs-mailer to refine when costs are known.*

To turn the unboxing into part of the sisterhood:

- **Outer parcel** — recycled kraft mailer in soft cream, simple wordmark stamp in maroon
- **Inner wrap** — unbleached tissue paper, sealed with a paper sticker that carries the Core Belief line in small print
- **Handwritten note** — every order, on a small card. First-time buyers receive a longer note; returning customers, a shorter one
- **Care card** — practical wash/iron instructions in the Warm Sister voice ("This one likes a cool iron and a gentle hand.")
- **Scent** — a faint signature scent on the tissue (oud-light or white florals — to decide)
- **Repeat-customer touch** — the second parcel includes a small bonus: a fabric scrap from the next collection, a dua card, or a Friday-night note from the founder

The unboxing should feel like opening a parcel from a sister, not from a brand.

## 10. Brand Architecture & Naming  ✓

*Locked as the working recommendation. Specific chapter and product names can evolve as collections are designed.*

- **Collection naming pattern** — *chapters of the strive*. Each collection a stage on the path:
  - *Niyyah* (intention)
  - *Sabr* (patience)
  - *Hijrah* (the turning)
  - *Tawakkul* (trust)
  - *Sakeenah* (stillness)
  - *Shukr* (gratitude)
  Each chapter is a small lesson the wardrobe carries. This pattern grows with the brand for years and never runs out of meaning.
- **Product naming pattern** — *Recommendation: Malay names* (*Aisyah*, *Hawa*, *Khadijah*, *Lila*). They carry meaning, they're hers to speak about by name to her sisters, and they keep the brand culturally rooted in KL.
- **The community** — *"sisters in the strive"*, or simply *"the strive"* used as a noun (*"join the strive"*, *"a letter for the strive"*). Avoids "tribe" / "community" / "family" — all overused in fashion.
- **The signature silhouette** — *Recommendation: yes.* One piece that returns every collection, in a new fabric and a new name. The *KADH constant*. Gives returning customers a fixed point and lets the brand mark seasons by what changes around it.
